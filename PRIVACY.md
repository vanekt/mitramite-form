# Privacy Policy

**Unofficial Mitramite Status Form** is a browser extension that restores the trámite status lookup form on https://mitramite.renaper.gob.ar/.

## What the extension does with data

- When you enter a trámite number and press the button, the extension sends it to the website's own endpoint (`busqueda.php` on mitramite.renaper.gob.ar) together with a reCAPTCHA token generated by the site, exactly as the site's original form did. The response is shown on the page.
- Trámite numbers you look up, and the last known status for each, are saved in your browser's local storage for that site, only to offer them as suggestions the next time. You can remove any of them with the × button next to it in the suggestions list.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, transmit or sell any data to the developer or to any third party.
- It has no servers, analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not request any browser permissions and only runs on https://mitramite.renaper.gob.ar/.
